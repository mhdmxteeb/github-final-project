# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behavior and taking appropriate and fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior. They may remove or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project or its community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated, and appropriate action will be taken while respecting the reporter's privacy.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may use the following guidelines when responding to violations:

1. **Correction:** A private warning explaining the inappropriate behavior.
2. **Warning:** A formal warning for a violation or repeated behavior.
3. **Temporary Ban:** Temporary restriction from project participation for serious or repeated violations.
4. **Permanent Ban:** Permanent removal from project participation for a sustained pattern of unacceptable behavior.
