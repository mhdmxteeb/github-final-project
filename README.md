# Simple Interest Calculator

This project calculates simple interest given the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period in years.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

## Input

- `p`: Principal amount
- `t`: Time period in years
- `r`: Annual rate of interest

## Output

The simple interest calculated using the formula above.

## Example

For a principal of 1000, an annual interest rate of 5%, and a time period of 2 years:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

## Usage

Enter the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period to calculate simple interest.
